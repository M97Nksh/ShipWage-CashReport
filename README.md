Ship Wage & Cash Report

A simple tool for ships’ officers to handle the monthly crew accounts in one place. It calculates each crew member’s salary from days worked, subtracts cash advances and cigarette purchases, and shows the final amount to pay. It also keeps track of the bonded store (cigarettes), master’s presents, and ship cash on board.

Active and disembarked crew with automatic day count from join or sign-off dates
Settlement list for all crew, ready to print or save as PDF
Bonded store and ship cash ledgers, with carry-forward to the next month
Works in English, Georgian, Turkish and Russian
Runs offline on your computer, and your data stays with you, with backup and CSV export

One-line version: Crew payroll, expenses and bonded store in one place, offline and in four languages.
